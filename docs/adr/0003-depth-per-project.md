# Each project is documented either in full or as a black box

Every project folder's index declares a depth:

- **Full depth** applies to projects the KB's owners work in. It covers internals, architecture, and gotchas.
- **Black box** applies to projects owned by someone else. It records only the integration surface: what the project exposes, how the documented projects depend on it, who owns it, and where its repo lives.

A KB exists to hold its owners' own tribal knowledge. Another team's internals drift out of date fast when nobody here works in that repo. They are also one clone away when they're actually needed. The integration surface is the part that does belong here, because the KB's owners are the ones who have to keep their side of it working.



The cost is that some questions will hit the black-box edge. When they do, the right answer is a pointer to the owning team or repo, not a guess.
